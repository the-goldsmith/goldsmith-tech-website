Privacy notice.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This notice explains what happens when you browse the Goldsmith portfolio, use Ananta, or send a project inquiry. It is website information, not legal advice.

Project inquiries

If this site is hosted on Netlify, the inquiry form sends the name, email address, selected service, message, and privacy-notice acknowledgment to Netlify Forms, where the site owner can review submissions. Email notifications depend on the site's Netlify settings. Netlify processes the submission as the website hosting/form provider and may process technical request information to operate and protect the service. Review Netlify's privacy notice.

The form includes a basic anti-spam honeypot. Do not send passwords, payment details, confidential material, or sensitive personal information. If the hosted form is unavailable, you may email services@goldsmith.gold.in.

When you open the website directly from your computer, the form opens your email application instead of submitting online. Your email provider then handles that message under its own terms.

Ananta assistant

Ananta answers catalog and FAQ questions from information stored in this website. When a question needs broader guidance and the Copilot backend is configured, the current question and up to five recent chat messages are sent to a server-side Netlify Function and forwarded to GitHub Copilot. The site does not save chat history. Netlify and GitHub may process requests under their respective terms and policies; review Netlify's privacy notice and GitHub's privacy statement. If Copilot is unavailable or not configured, Ananta uses its local site guide instead. Do not enter passwords, personal, confidential, or otherwise sensitive information.

Optional analytics and cookies

Google Analytics is currently not configured on this site because a measurement ID has not been supplied. No Google Analytics script or analytics cookies are loaded. If analytics is configured later, it will only load after you explicitly choose “Allow analytics” in Cookie settings. Your choice is stored in this browser so it can be remembered. To change it from this page, return to the portfolio and use Cookie settings in its footer.

The site does not currently set analytics cookies. External fonts and images may be requested directly from their providers when the page loads; those providers may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. This site does not control their processing. Where possible, company-owned images and locally hosted assets can reduce those requests.

Retention and your choices

Website form submissions may be retained by the hosting/form provider and the recipient team. A specific deletion schedule or request process has not been provided for this site. Contact services@goldsmith.gold.in with a privacy question or request; the team will review it.

Privacy, data-protection, and consumer rights depend on where you live and how the site is operated. The site owner should review this notice and configure provider retention and contact details before publishing.
