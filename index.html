      04HELP & FAQ

      Quick answers.
      Real people, too.

      Have a question about what we build or how to get started? Browse the answers or ask Ananta.

      What can Goldsmith help me build?+

      We work on custom software, applied AI and computer vision, responsible security systems, and early-stage technology prototypes.

      What kinds of software do you build?+

      We work on custom digital products, business tools, applied AI, computer vision, and responsible technology concepts. Each project is shaped around its goals and users.

      How do I start a project or collaboration?+

      Send a short description of the challenge, your goals, and any timeline using the contact form or email. The website does not list a response timeframe.

      Do you publish fixed pricing or delivery timelines?+

      No fixed rates or delivery dates are published. A project-specific estimate and milestones can only be discussed after the requirements, constraints, and scope are understood.

      What technology languages do you work with?+

      Goldsmith's stated areas include Java, PHP, and Python, along with web platforms, applied AI, and connected-system concepts. The right tools depend on project requirements and feasibility.

      Is the robotics system available now?+

      No. The robot shown is a concept study exploring inspection, situational awareness, and human-robot collaboration. It is not a deployed or weaponized product.

      What are the AI drone concepts for?+

      Concept applications include supervised site inspection, mapping, and environmental observation. They are exploratory ideas, not a deployed product; no weaponization or autonomous targeting is involved.
